Techopedia Level 12 marks the twelfth edition of IEEE SIES GST's annual technical fest.

The theme revolves around the exciting world of gaming.

Participation increases your chances of winning cash prizes, coupons, goodies, national-level certificates, and more!

The Registration Fee is Rs. 80 for a team of two in Squabble, Inquisitive & Eureka, and Rs. 180 for a team of three in Warlash.

On-day registration is available for WarLash!
